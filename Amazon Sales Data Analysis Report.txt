Amazon Sales Data Analysis Report

Problem Statement
Analyze and Provide Insights on Amazon Sales Report
Problem Description
The dataset contains information about Amazon sales transactions including order ID, date, status, fulfilment method, sales channel, product category, size, quantity, amount, and shipping details. The objective is to perform a comprehensive analysis and extract actionable insights for business decision-making.

Dataset Link
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YrjYKtS1WHmINL6eafRsrDzrZaw2_WvX/view


Key Objectives
Sales Overview: Analyze overall sales trends and patterns over time.
Product Analysis: Identify popular product categories, sizes, and quantities.
Fulfillment Analysis: Evaluate effectiveness of fulfillment methods.
Customer Segmentation: Segment customers based on behavior and location.
Geographical Analysis: Study sales distribution across regions.
Business Insights: Provide actionable recommendations for growth.


Deliverables
Comprehensive analysis report with findings and recommendations.
Visualizations including charts and graphs.
Insights on product preferences and customer behavior.
Recommendations for improving sales, inventory, and customer service.
